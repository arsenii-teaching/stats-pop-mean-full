Discussion questions

  1. Describe the population distribution of xG. Is μ = a parameter or a statistic? Is your squad's x̄?
  2. Set n = 1 and simulate 1000 trials. Why does the sampling distribution of x̄ look just like the population distribution?
  3. Simulate 1000 trials for each preset n. Is the mean of the x̄ values close to μ every time? What does this say about x̄ as an unbiased estimator?
  4. Compare the SD of the x̄ values with σ/√n. Going from n = 5 to n = 20, by what factor does the spread shrink? Why does halving the SD need 4× the sample size?
  5. Describe the shape of the sampling distribution at n = 5, 11 and 30. When is the Normal model reasonable? Connect your answer to the CLT.
  6. Interpret the SD of x̄ for n = 30: “In SRSs of size ___, the sample mean xG typically varies by about ___ from the true mean μ = ___.”
  7. Check the 10% condition for n = 40 and for n = 100. Set n = (the whole population): why is σ/√n now wrong about the spread?
